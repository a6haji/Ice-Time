Four tabs, one season

Everything you need on the ice, and nothing you don't.

Ice Time is built around the way you actually play — four focused tabs that turn a quick post-game tap into a full picture of your season.

Skates

Log a game in three quick steps — game type, score, and your stat line. Goals, assists, plus-minus, notes, done.

Rinks

Live rink discovery finds where you're skating. Save your regulars, set a home rink, or suggest one that's missing.

Friends

Invite the bench with a QR code or link. See friends' recent stats and go head-to-head at the rinks you share.

Stats

Season and career views, computed live — points, win rate, points per game, streaks, and rinks visited.

Rinks

Know every sheet of ice you skate.

Rink discovery is live — search by name or find what's nearby, no seeded list to go stale. Voice search means you can find a rink with your gloves still on. Every rink page shows your stat line there, who else skates it, and how you stack up head-to-head.

  • Live search and nearby discovery powered by Maps
  • Hands-free voice search
  • Save your regulars and set a home rink
  • Suggest a missing rink for everyone

Stats

Watch your season take shape.

Every stat is computed live from your skates — nothing to update by hand. Flip between season and career, set your own season window, and see the numbers that actually tell the story: win rate, points per game, hot streaks, and how this year compares to last.

  • Season and career views, always up to date
  • Points, win rate, points-per-game and streaks
  • Custom season ranges — default Sep 1 to Apr 30
  • A friends leaderboard to keep it honest

Bring the bench

One code adds the whole line.

Share a QR code or an invite link and you're instantly connected — no requests to approve, no friend-of-a-friend maze. A live ticker keeps everyone's goals and assists front and centre, and every teammate's card shows their number, position and shooting hand.

Private by design

Your season is yours. Full stop.

Ice Time earns its stats the honest way. No advertising, no third-party analytics, no data sold to anyone — and your location is only ever read while the app is open.

No ads, no trackers

No advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or crash reporters. Nothing follows you around.

Foreground-only location

Location is used only while you're in the app, to find rinks and detect the one you're at. Raw GPS is never stored.

Never sold

We don't sell your personal information — to anyone, for any reason.

Stored in Canada

Your account and activity data live on Canadian servers, encrypted in transit, protected by row-level security.

How it works

From first skate to full season in three steps.

  1. 1

    Set up your profile

    Add your name, position, jersey number and shooting hand. Takes a minute, then you're on the ice.

  2. 2

    Log every skate

    After each game, a few taps captures the score and your stat line while the rink detects itself.

  3. 3

    Watch it add up

    Season summaries, career totals and a friends leaderboard build themselves as you play.
